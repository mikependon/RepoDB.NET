# Interfaces (Telemetry)

---

Interfaces for customizing how telemetry is captured and delivered. [IPublisherRepository](/interface/telemetry/ipublisherrepository) lets you control how [TelemetryItem](/class/telemetryitem) objects are shipped to your insights solution.
