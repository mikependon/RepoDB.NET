# Enumerations (Db2)

---

Enumerations used by the [Db2 bulk operations](/operation/db2). [Db2BulkImportIdentityBehavior](/enumeration/db2/db2bulkimportidentitybehavior) controls whether newly generated identity values are returned back to the entities after [BulkInsert](/operation/db2/bulkinsert) or [BulkMerge](/operation/db2/bulkmerge), and [Db2BulkImportPseudoTableType](/enumeration/db2/db2bulkimportpseudotabletype) controls the kind of staging table created to stage the bulk-imported data.
