# Enumerations (Vertica)

---

Enumerations used by the [Vertica bulk operations](/operation/vertica). [VerticaBulkImportIdentityBehavior](/enumeration/vertica/verticabulkimportidentitybehavior) controls whether newly generated identity values are returned back to the entities after [BulkInsert](/operation/vertica/bulkinsert) or [BulkMerge](/operation/vertica/bulkmerge), and [VerticaBulkImportPseudoTableType](/enumeration/vertica/verticabulkimportpseudotabletype) controls the kind of staging table created to stage the bulk-imported data.
