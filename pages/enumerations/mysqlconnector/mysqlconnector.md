# Enumerations (MySqlConnector)

---

Enumerations used by the [MySqlConnector bulk operations](/operation/mysqlconnector). [MySqlConnectorBulkImportIdentityBehavior](/enumeration/mysqlconnector/mysqlconnectorbulkimportidentitybehavior) controls whether newly generated identity values are returned back to the entities after [BulkInsert](/operation/mysqlconnector/bulkinsert) or [BulkMerge](/operation/mysqlconnector/bulkmerge), and [MySqlConnectorBulkImportPseudoTableType](/enumeration/mysqlconnector/mysqlconnectorbulkimportpseudotabletype) controls the kind of staging table created to stage the bulk-imported data.
