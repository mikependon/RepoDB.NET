# Enumerations (EnterpriseDB)

---

Enumerations used by the [EnterpriseDB bulk operations](/operation/enterprisedb). [EDBBulkImportIdentityBehavior](/enumeration/enterprisedb/edbbulkimportidentitybehavior) controls whether newly generated identity values are returned back to the entities after [BulkInsert](/operation/enterprisedb#bulkinsert) or [BulkMerge](/operation/enterprisedb#bulkmerge), and [EDBBulkImportPseudoTableType](/enumeration/enterprisedb/edbbulkimportpseudotabletype) controls the kind of staging table created to stage the bulk-imported data.
