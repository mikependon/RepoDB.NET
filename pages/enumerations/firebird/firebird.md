# Enumerations (Firebird)

---

Enumerations used by the [Firebird bulk operations](https://www.nuget.org/packages/RepoDb.Firebird.BulkOperations). [FirebirdBulkImportIdentityBehavior](/enumeration/firebird/firebirdbulkimportidentitybehavior) controls whether newly generated identity values are returned back to the entities after `BulkInsert` or `BulkMerge`, and [FirebirdBulkImportPseudoTableType](/enumeration/firebird/firebirdbulkimportpseudotabletype) controls the kind of staging table created to stage the bulk-imported data.
