# Enumerations (SAP HANA)

---

Enumerations used by the [SAP HANA bulk operations](/operation/saphana). [SapHanaBulkImportIdentityBehavior](/enumeration/saphana/saphanabulkimportidentitybehavior) controls whether newly generated identity values are returned back to the entities after [BulkInsert](/operation/saphana/bulkinsert) or [BulkMerge](/operation/saphana/bulkmerge), and [SapHanaBulkImportPseudoTableType](/enumeration/saphana/saphanabulkimportpseudotabletype) controls the kind of staging table created to stage the bulk-imported data.
